# OneWay Watch — Emblem / Visual Identity Prompt

Use the supplied black-and-white reference images as visual inspiration for the emblem language, not as a pixel-for-pixel copy.

## Master prompt

Design a premium Japanese-inspired emblem for **ONEWAY WATCH**, a modern anime and manga streaming/reading platform. Create a **circular crest / mon (家紋)-inspired mark** with a strong outer ring and a highly symmetrical central composition. The central icon should combine a **stylized lotus blossom** with an abstract **guardian / oni-inspired mask or protective spirit**, subtly incorporating a small celestial symbol above it. Use flowing cloud motifs, flame-like curves, ornamental linework, and balanced negative space to create the feeling of an ancient seal protecting a library of stories.

Visual language: **bold black ink line art, Japanese woodblock print influence, traditional tattoo flash discipline, mon/emblem geometry, sacred seal aesthetic, elegant ornamental symmetry, crisp vector-like edges, hand-inked character, high contrast, iconic silhouette, refined negative space**.

Composition: perfectly centered, radial symmetry, circular outer border, central lotus/guardian symbol, secondary ornamental clouds/flames on the horizontal axis, small geometric celestial mark at the top, no perspective distortion, no text inside the emblem, no unnecessary micro-detail.

Material/style: monochrome black ink on warm ivory/off-white paper, subtle paper grain, slightly imperfect hand-printed ink edges, museum-quality Japanese printmaking feel, premium streetwear crest, sophisticated rather than cartoonish.

The mark must work as a website favicon, app icon, loading symbol, merchandise emblem, watermark, and hero branding. Make the silhouette recognizable at small sizes.

## Negative prompt

photorealistic, 3D render, glossy chrome, metallic logo, neon, gradients, rainbow colors, soft airbrush, excessive shading, realistic face, western heraldry, medieval European shield, text, letters, watermark, mockup, background objects, asymmetry, messy composition, thin fragile lines, low contrast, generic AI logo, copied existing logo, trademark imitation.

## UI application prompt

Apply this emblem to a premium anime/manga streaming website called **ONEWAY WATCH**. Build a dark cinematic interface around the monochrome crest: near-black background, ivory typography, restrained crimson accent, subtle paper/ink texture, Japanese editorial grid, large atmospheric hero imagery, crisp anime cards, elegant navigation, smooth motion, and generous negative space. The emblem should appear as the primary brand mark in the navbar, loading screen, player overlay, manga reader, and favicon. Keep the site sophisticated and modern rather than kitschy or overly cyberpunk.
